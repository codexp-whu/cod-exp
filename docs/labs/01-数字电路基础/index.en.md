# Digital Logic Fundamentals
**For every lab from here on, read the explanatory part of the corresponding chapter of *CPU Design in Practice: LoongArch Edition* first, then come back to these notes for the lab requirements and complete them.**

!!! warning
    **The lab notes on this site are the authoritative statement of all lab requirements. Do not follow the lab requirements given in *CPU Design in Practice: LoongArch Edition*!**

!!! info "Reference in the Book"
    The labs in this section correspond to [Chapter 3, Fundamentals of Digital Logic Circuit Design](https://bookdown.org/loongson/_book3/chapter-digital-logic.html) of *CPU Design in Practice: LoongArch Edition*, and they form the foundation for every CPU lab that follows.

Before you start designing a CPU, you need to be fluent in writing, simulating and programming three kinds of basic building block: the register file, RAM, and a complete small digital logic system.

The three practical tasks in this section take you through that process.
